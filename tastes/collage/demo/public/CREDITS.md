# Collage intro: image sources

Files in `raw/` are the downloads as fetched (the May 8 newspaper page was converted from JP2 to JPEG with `sips`). Files in this folder are the crops and cut-outs. Cut-outs were made with rembg (isnet-general-use) or hand-built masks (see notes), then checked on mid-grey at full size.

## 2. factory (cut-outs, best first)

### factory-1-amana-woolen-mill.png (3895 x 1570)
- Title: Middle Amana Woolen Mill, ca. 1900
- Institution: Hamilton College Library, Rare Books and Special Collections, Communal Societies Collection, Amana Society Collection (ama-pho-050). A c.1970s print from an original glass-plate negative of c.1900.
- Internet Archive id: yhm-spe-ama-pho-050
- URL: https://archive.org/details/yhm-spe-ama-pho-050
- File: https://archive.org/download/yhm-spe-ama-pho-050/yhm-spe-ama-pho-050_s001.jpg (6147 x 4934)
- Licence: not stated on the item (the record says to contact Hamilton College about copyright). The photograph is about 1900, so it is public domain by age, but the item carries no PD mark. Confirm before shipping.
- Raw: `raw/factory-amana-woolen-mill-c1900.jpg`
- Notes: black and white. Sky is keyed by brightness, the ground line and distant haze are cut by a hand-drawn polygon. The bottom edge is a soft fade, and the left outbuilding is clipped at the photo edge.

### factory-2-blissfield-sugar-factory.png (2219 x 716)
