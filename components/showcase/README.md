# Showcase components

Ten motion-graphics components for presenting an app: three tilted phones, a phone pitch, a screen tunnel, a laptop, a phone tap, a checkout push, a balance count and a button-to-app reveal. Device frames and the sample app ("Stride", a made-up fitness app) are drawn here;
nothing is a real product. Every on-screen string, colour and font is a prop (Studio builds controls from the zod schemas).
Screens are either a built-in drawn one (`hero`, `dashboard`, `chart`, `plan`; the laptop has `desk`) or a path to an image
under `public/` (`public/photos` links to `components/_assets/photos`, NASA public domain, see CREDITS.md there).

Render from the repo root (`<Id>` is the composition id):

    node ../kinetograph/packages/cli/dist/cli.js still components/showcase/index.tsx <frame> --comp <Id> -o components/showcase/out/x.png
    node ../kinetograph/packages/cli/dist/cli.js render components/showcase/index.tsx --comp <Id> -o components/showcase/out/<id>.mp4

Type-check: `npx --no-install tsc -p tsconfig.json` in this folder.

## Principles used

- **ThreePhone**: staging (one screen at a time), anticipation (a dip before each move), slow in and out with overshoot, arcs (banked sweep), motion blur on the fast part, solid drawing (one light, real 3D yaw).
- **PhonePitch**: anticipation, follow through (words overshoot right and settle), overlap (the headline leaves as the camera lifts), staging, timing (replies at human intervals, typing dots before the last).
- **ScreenTunnel**: staging, slow in and out (exponential ease-out pull), arcs (pan of the rig centre), overlap (headline borrows the ease, none of the travel), follow through (the shove settles), solid drawing (a true box tunnel, mouth 2.35x the aperture).
- **LaptopOpen**: staging, depth of field (a focus pull as the lens arrives), slow in and out (one continuous fitted pose table), follow through (the lens keeps turning through the hold), solid drawing (lid and deck planes hinged at 118 degrees, 16 inch proportions).
- **ScreenTouch**: staging, anticipation and follow through (press is a quick scale, flash outlives it), slow in and out (the first fall accelerates in), timing (closing lines climb slowly).
- **CheckoutPush**: anticipation (a held beat before the tilt), slow in and fast out with motion blur, solid drawing (pose fitted to the silhouette), staging.
- **VaultCount**: timing (the counter starts hesitant and settles), depth from blur and contrast, staging (only the number changes while the cards move).
- **BuildOut**: staging, slow in and out, anticipation (pointer arrives edge-on, turns flat, presses), overlap and stagger (blocks draw in), follow through.
- **LaunchFilm**: timing (a typed line pauses where a person would), staging (one idea per beat, always centred), anticipation and follow through (the mark swells and shrinks before it slides, the letter overshoots before it yaws), overlap (the dashboard resolves while the dots clear), solid drawing (one perspective for the extrusion), secondary action (drifting squares, motion blur on the cuts).
- **PromptIntro**: anticipation (the arrow dips before it launches), slow in and out (every move is the reference's measured ease), overlap (the tint trails the typing, the dots trail the line), staging (one line at a time), follow through (the answer settles from 1.29x).
- **AppLaunch**: staging (one idea per beat), anticipation (the arrow dips, the button swells before it morphs), slow in and out (every beat runs on the reference's measured eases), overlap (chips 10 frames apart, screen layers drifting at their own rates), follow through (the app nodes overshoot 4%), secondary action (the glow under every beat), timing (typing gaps grow like a person's).
- **RecapDial**: timing (a 6-frame snap and a long hold, so each moment reads), anticipation and follow through (each snap overshoots about 7% and settles), staging (light falls off with distance from the middle row), arcs, secondary action (stickers turn with the dial), motion blur on the snap only.

## Reference credit

PhonePitch, ScreenTunnel, LaptopOpen, ScreenTouch, CheckoutPush, VaultCount and BuildOut are timed shot by shot to measured demo scenes (phone-pitch, screen-wall, laptop-open, tap-through, checkout-push, vault-count, build-out), measured frame by frame; no source was used and no brand, copy or asset is copied. ThreePhone has no reference. LaunchFilm follows the layout and timing of a product-launch film reference (measured frame by frame); its words, marks and icons are our own, its pictures are public-domain artworks and runner photos from Wikimedia Commons (credited in `_assets/photos/CREDITS.md`), and all are props. RecapDial follows the layout, dial geometry (measured: a 1026 px circle, 19 degrees per label) and snap timing of a vertical "recap" template video; its words, stickers and photos are our own and are all props. PromptIntro is the opening of a SaaS launch reel measured with the kinetograph-taste-clone skill: its motion is the reference's per-frame tables; its words, font and glow are ours and are props. AppLaunch keeps that reel's taste (its glow, glass, salmon sweep, coral letter pops, guide lines, measured eases and hard cuts) and its opening, chat-bar and closing beats on the reference's frames; 0:09-0:16 is a new design that tells the meeting's story, and all content, marks and icons are ours.
