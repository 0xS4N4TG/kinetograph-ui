# Photos

Photos from the Internet Archive, used in the mock photo feed. Re-download and resize them with
`tools/fetch-photos.sh` (long edge 1600 px, JPEG; a few are also cropped to drop a caption strip or print border).

Rule for this folder: only works that are clearly public domain in the US. That means US government works (here, all
NASA), works published before 1930 (the 95-year US term has run out for everything published before 1930 as of
2026), or museum open-access items the museum itself marks CC0. The dates below are the dates on the archive.org items.
