# Archive credits

All items are US public domain: published before 1930 (Edison films and Century Magazine 1894 are long out of copyright), or works of the US National Park Service. Clips are trimmed to 3-8 s, padded to 1920x1080 H.264 (CRF 16, no audio) at native fps. The sources are low-resolution scans (320x240 to 1440x1080), so they are upscaled with Lanczos.
