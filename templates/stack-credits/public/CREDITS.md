# Asset credits (stack-credits)

Every file here is a public-domain or CC0 photograph. Originals are in `raw/`. Prepared files were converted to
greyscale (autocontrast) and cropped to the picture area; cut-outs (`*.png`) were made with rembg (isnet-general-use)
and cleaned by hand-written alpha edits. No image was generated.

Licences used
- Met Open Access: Creative Commons Zero (CC0), item flagged `isPublicDomain: true` in the Met API.
- Cleveland Museum of Art Open Access: CC0 (`share_license_status: CC0`).
- DOCUMERICA (US EPA, 1971-77): US federal government work, public domain. Served from the Internet Archive item
  `documerica_photos` (rights mark: Public Domain Mark 1.0). Member path: `DOCUMERICA.zip/<photographer>/<file>`.
- Internet Archive item `psx-20230817-231449`: photographer Stefan Klein released the photos under CC0 1.0.
