# Posters

Scans from the Internet Archive, used by `posters.tsx`. Re-download and resize with `tools/fetch-posters.sh`
(long edge 2000 px, JPEG; the KLM poster is also cropped to drop its blank bottom strip).

Rule for this folder: only works that are clearly public domain in the US. That means US government works, or
works published before 1930 (the 95-year US term has run out for everything published before 1930 as of 2026). The
dates below are publication dates. Nothing here is under a licence that needs a credit, but the artists are named
anyway.

The Spyker, Rotterdamsche Lloyd, KLM and Automobielen posters may still be protected in their home countries (their
artists may have died less than 70 years ago). They are public domain in the US, so the film is fine for US-facing
use; swap them out before wider distribution.
